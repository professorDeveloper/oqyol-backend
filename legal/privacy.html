Amal qiladi: Oqyol mobil ilovasi (yo‘lovchilar uchun)

Ushbu Maxfiylik siyosati Oqyol mobil ilovasi (“Ilova”) sizning shaxsiy ma’lumotlaringizni qanday yig‘ishi, ulardan foydalanishi, ularni ulashishi va himoya qilishini tushuntiradi. Ilovadan foydalanish orqali siz ushbu hujjatda bayon etilgan amaliyotlarga rozilik bildirasiz.

Oqyol yo‘lovchilar va haydovchilarni shaharlararo va shahar ichi safarlar uchun bog‘laydi. Biz faqat xizmatni ko‘rsatish uchun zarur ma’lumotlarni yig‘amiz, ularni hech kimga sotmaymiz va reklama tarmoqlariga bermaymiz. To‘lov kartasi raqamlarini saqlamaymiz.

1. Umumiy ma’lumot

Oqyol — foydalanuvchilarga shaharlararo va shahar ichi safarlarni tashkil qilishda yordam beradigan mobil ilova. Biz sizning maxfiyligingizni jiddiy qabul qilamiz va faqat xizmatni taqdim etish, uni yaxshilash hamda xavfsizligini ta’minlash uchun zarur bo‘lgan ma’lumotlarni qayta ishlaymiz.

Ma’lumotlar nazoratchisi: Oqyol jamoasi. Bog‘lanish: azmv21@gmail.com.

2. Biz yig‘adigan ma’lumotlar

Biz faqat xizmat uchun zarur bo‘lgan ma’lumotlarni yig‘amiz. Quyida toifalar bo‘yicha ko‘rsatilgan:

ToifaNimalarMaqsad
Hisob ma’lumotlariTelefon raqami, ism, profil rasmi (avatar)Ro‘yxatdan o‘tish va profilni ko‘rsatish
AutentifikatsiyaSMS-kod (OTP), sessiya tokenlariHisobga xavfsiz kirish
JoylashuvAniq va taxminiy GPS joylashuvi (ilova ochiq bo‘lganda)Olib ketish/yetkazish nuqtalari, xaritada ko‘rsatish
Safar ma’lumotlariMarshrutlar, hududlar va tumanlar, buyurtmalar, takliflar, reytinglar, nizolarSafarlarni tashkil qilish va tarixini yuritish
Texnik ma’lumotlarIP-manzil, qurilma turi, ilova versiyasi, jurnallarXavfsizlik, nosozliklarni tuzatish, barqarorlik
MurojaatlarBizga yuborgan xatlaringiz mazmuniQo‘llab-quvvatlash va savollarga javob

Biz yig‘maydigan ma’lumotlar

  • To‘lov kartasi raqamlari yoki bank ma’lumotlari
  • Kontaktlar ro‘yxati, SMS yoki qo‘ng‘iroqlar tarixi
  • Ilova yopiq bo‘lganda fon (background) joylashuvi

Ilova kirish tokeni va sozlamalarni qurilmangizda mahalliy saqlaydi — bu ma’lumot serverga yuborilmaydi.

3. Ma’lumotlardan qanday foydalanamiz

  • Xizmatni ko‘rsatish: hisob yaratish, safarlarni moslashtirish, buyurtma va takliflarni boshqarish.
  • Aloqa: safar holati, tasdiqlash kodlari va muhim bildirishnomalarni yuborish.
  • Xavfsizlik: firibgarlik va suiiste’molning oldini olish, hisoblarni himoya qilish.
  • Yaxshilash: xatoliklarni tahlil qilish va xizmat sifatini oshirish.
  • Qonuniy majburiyatlar: amaldagi qonunchilik talablariga rioya qilish.

Qayta ishlash asoslari: siz bilan tuzilgan xizmat shartnomasi, sizning roziligingiz (masalan, joylashuv), qonuniy manfaatlar (xavfsizlik) va qonuniy majburiyatlar.

4. Joylashuv ma’lumotlari

Ilova joylashuv ruxsatlaridan (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) faqat siz ilovadan foydalanayotganda foydalanadi — safar nuqtalarini aniqlash va ularni xaritada ko‘rsatish uchun. Biz ilova yopiq bo‘lganda joylashuvni kuzatmaymiz.

Joylashuv ruxsatini istalgan vaqtda qurilma sozlamalaridan o‘chirib qo‘yishingiz mumkin. Bunda xaritaga bog‘liq funksiyalar cheklanishi mumkin.

5. Ma’lumotlarni ulashish

Biz sizning ma’lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz. Ma’lumotlar faqat quyidagi hollarda ulashiladi:

  • Haydovchi va yo‘lovchi o‘rtasida: safarni amalga oshirish uchun zarur ism, reyting va olib ketish/yetkazish nuqtasi.
  • Xizmat ko‘rsatuvchilar bilan: faqat bizning ko‘rsatmamiz asosida ishlovchi texnik provayderlar (6-bo‘limga qarang).
  • Qonun talab qilganda: vakolatli organlarning qonuniy so‘rovi bo‘yicha yoki huquqlarni himoya qilish uchun.

6. Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

Ilova ishlashi uchun biz quyidagi ishonchli provayderlardan foydalanamiz:

ProvayderMaqsad
DigitalOceanServer va ma’lumotlar bazasini joylashtirish (Singapur)
CloudflareHTTPS, xavfsizlik va trafikni yetkazib berish; fayl saqlash (R2)
MapboxXarita va xarita plitkalari

Ushbu provayderlar ma’lumotlarni faqat xizmatni ta’minlash uchun, o‘z maxfiylik majburiyatlariga muvofiq qayta ishlaydi.

7. Ma’lumotlarni saqlash muddati

Ma’lumotlar xizmatni ko‘rsatish uchun zarur bo‘lgan muddat davomida yoki siz o‘chirishni so‘ramaguningizcha saqlanadi. Hisobingizni o‘chirganingizdan so‘ng, ma’lumotlar 30 kun ichida o‘chiriladi. Qonun talab qilgan minimal yozuvlar belgilangan muddatgacha saqlanishi mumkin.

8. Xavfsizlik

Biz ma’lumotlaringizni himoya qilish uchun oqilona texnik va tashkiliy choralarni qo‘llaymiz: tarmoq orqali barcha aloqa HTTPS/TLS shifrlash bilan amalga oshiriladi, serverlarga kirish cheklangan va himoyalangan, kirish esa SMS-kod (OTP) orqali tasdiqlanadi. Shunga qaramay, internetda hech qanday usul 100% xavfsiz emasligini eslab qoling.

9. Sizning huquqlaringiz

  • Kirish: biz saqlayotgan ma’lumotlaringizni so‘rash.
  • Tuzatish: noto‘g‘ri ma’lumotlarni to‘g‘rilash (profil orqali).
  • O‘chirish: hisob va ma’lumotlaringizni o‘chirish (10-bo‘lim).
  • Rozilikni qaytarib olish: masalan, joylashuv ruxsatini o‘chirish.
  • Shikoyat: ma’lumotlarni himoya qilish bo‘yicha tegishli organga murojaat qilish.

Huquqlaringizdan foydalanish uchun azmv21@gmail.com manziliga yozing.

10. Hisob va ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish

Siz istalgan vaqtda hisobingizni va u bilan bog‘liq barcha ma’lumotlarni o‘chirishingiz mumkin:

  • Ilova ichida: Profil → Hisobni o‘chirish → telefoningizga kelgan SMS-kod bilan tasdiqlang. Hisob darhol o‘chiriladi.
  • Email orqali: ro‘yxatdan o‘tgan telefon raqamingizni azmv21@gmail.com manziliga yuboring.

Batafsil ko‘rsatma: Ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish sahifasi.

11. Bolalar maxfiyligi

Ilova 13 yoshdan kichik bolalarga mo‘ljallanmagan va biz ataylab ulardan ma’lumot yig‘maymiz. Agar bunday ma’lumot yig‘ilgan bo‘lsa, biz uni o‘chiramiz.

12. Xalqaro ma’lumot uzatish

Serverlarimiz Singapurda joylashgan. Ilovadan foydalanish orqali ma’lumotlaringiz xizmat ko‘rsatish uchun shu serverlarga uzatilishi va u yerda qayta ishlanishiga rozilik bildirasiz.

13. Siyosatdagi o‘zgarishlar

Biz ushbu siyosatni vaqti-vaqti bilan yangilashimiz mumkin. Muhim o‘zgarishlar haqida ilova orqali yoki ushbu sahifada xabar beramiz, eng yuqorida esa sana yangilanadi.

14. Biz bilan bog‘lanish

Maxfiylik bo‘yicha savollar yoki so‘rovlar uchun: azmv21@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Applies to: the Oqyol mobile app (for passengers)

This Privacy Policy explains how the Oqyol mobile app (the “App”) collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this document.

Oqyol connects passengers and drivers for intercity and in-city trips. We collect only the data we need to provide the service, we never sell it, and we do not share it with advertising networks. We do not store payment card numbers.

1. Overview

Oqyol is a mobile app that helps users arrange intercity and in-city trips. We take your privacy seriously and process data only as needed to provide, improve, and secure the service.

Data controller: the Oqyol team. Contact: azmv21@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect only the data needed for the service. The categories are listed below:

CategoryWhatPurpose
Account dataPhone number, name, profile photo (avatar)Registration and displaying your profile
AuthenticationSMS code (OTP), session tokensSecure sign-in to your account
LocationPrecise and approximate GPS location (while the App is open)Pickup/drop-off points, showing them on the map
Trip dataRoutes, regions and districts, orders, offers, ratings, disputesArranging trips and keeping history
Technical dataIP address, device type, app version, logsSecurity, debugging, reliability
CommunicationsThe content of emails you send usSupport and answering your questions

What we do not collect

  • Payment card numbers or banking details
  • Your contacts, SMS, or call history
  • Background location when the App is closed

The App stores an auth token and preferences locally on your device — this is never sent to our servers.

3. How we use data

  • Provide the service: create your account, match trips, manage orders and offers.
  • Communicate: send trip status, confirmation codes, and important notifications.
  • Security: prevent fraud and abuse, and protect accounts.
  • Improve: analyze errors and improve service quality.
  • Legal obligations: comply with applicable law.

Our legal bases are the service contract with you, your consent (e.g. location), our legitimate interests (security), and legal obligations.

4. Location data

The App uses location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) only while you are using the App, to determine trip points and show them on the map. We do not track your location when the App is closed.

You can revoke the location permission at any time in your device settings; map-related features may then be limited.

5. How we share data

We do not sell your data. We share it only in these cases:

  • Between driver and passenger: the name, rating, and pickup/drop-off point needed to complete a trip.
  • With service providers: technical providers acting only on our instructions (see section 6).
  • When required by law: in response to lawful requests, or to protect rights.

6. Third-party services

To run the App, we rely on the following trusted providers:

ProviderPurpose
DigitalOceanServer and database hosting (Singapore)
CloudflareHTTPS, security and content delivery; file storage (R2)
MapboxMaps and map tiles

These providers process data only to provide the service, in line with their own privacy commitments.

7. Data retention

We retain data for as long as needed to provide the service, or until you request deletion. After you delete your account, data is removed within 30 days. Minimal records required by law may be retained for the required period.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards: all network communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption, server access is restricted and protected, and sign-in is verified with a one-time SMS code (OTP). That said, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Your rights

  • Access: request the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data (via your profile).
  • Deletion: delete your account and data (section 10).
  • Withdraw consent: e.g. revoke location permission.
  • Complaint: contact a relevant data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, email azmv21@gmail.com.

10. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account → confirm with the SMS code sent to your phone. Your account is deleted right away.
  • By email: send your registered phone number to azmv21@gmail.com.

Full instructions: Data Deletion page.

11. Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If such data is collected, we will delete it.

12. International data transfers

Our servers are located in Singapore. By using the App, you consent to your data being transferred to and processed on these servers to provide the service.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will announce material changes in the App or on this page, and the date at the top will be updated.
