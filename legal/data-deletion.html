Hisob va ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 3-iyun

← Maxfiylik siyosati

Oqyol foydalanuvchilari hisobini va shaxsiy ma’lumotlarini istalgan vaqtda o‘chirib tashlashlari mumkin. Quyidagi ikki usuldan birini tanlang.

Usul 1 — Ilova ichidan (tavsiya etiladi)

  1. Oqyol ilovasini oching.
  2. Profil bo‘limiga o‘ting.
  3. Hisobni o‘chirish tugmasini bosing.
  4. Telefoningizga kelgan SMS-kodni kiriting va tasdiqlang. Hisob va bog‘liq ma’lumotlar darhol o‘chiriladi.

Usul 2 — Email orqali so‘rov

Email: azmv21@gmail.com

Mavzu: «Hisobni o‘chirish — Oqyol»

Xat ichida: ro‘yxatdan o‘tgan telefon raqamingizni ko‘rsating (shaxsni tasdiqlash uchun).

Nima o‘chiriladi?

  • Hisob (telefon raqami, ism, profil rasmi)
  • Safar va buyurtma tarixi, marshrutlar, takliflar
  • Joylashuv yozuvlari
  • Reytinglar va sharhlar

So‘rov 30 kun ichida bajariladi. Qonun talab qilgan minimal yozuvlar (firibgarlikning oldini olish, buxgalteriya) belgilangan muddatgacha saqlanishi mumkin.

Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: June 3, 2026

← Privacy Policy

Oqyol users can delete their account and personal data at any time. Choose one of the two methods below.

Method 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Oqyol app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter the SMS code sent to your phone and confirm. Your account and associated data are deleted right away.

Method 2 — By email request

Email: azmv21@gmail.com

Subject: “Account deletion — Oqyol”

In the email: include your registered phone number so we can verify your identity.

What gets deleted?

  • Your account (phone number, name, profile photo)
  • Trip and order history, routes, and offers
  • Location records
  • Ratings and reviews

Requests are completed within 30 days. Minimal records required by law (such as fraud prevention or accounting) may be retained for the required period.
